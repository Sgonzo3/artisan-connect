How it works

Text the Artisan Connect number. The booking agent guides you from shop choice to confirmation.

  1. 01

    Start the chat

    Send a message to open the conversation. Say hi to begin.

  2. 02

    Choose a shop

    See available locations with sample prices and hours, or ask for a full tour of each menu.

  3. 03

    Pick services & time

    Name what you want — haircut, beard, packages — then choose a day and time that fits.

  4. 04

    Confirm

    Review the summary and reply YES. A booking draft is sent for the shop to confirm.
